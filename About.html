About Bright Junior School

We are committed to nurturing disciplined, knowledgeable and responsible future leaders.

OUR HISTORY

Our Journey

Bright Junior School was established with the vision of providing quality secondary education to learners in Wakiso and beyond.

Over the years the school has grown into a respected institution known for academic excellence, discipline, leadership and innovation.

Today, we continue to inspire learners to achieve their dreams while maintaining strong moral values.

Our Vision

To become a centre of excellence producing responsible, innovative and globally competitive citizens.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through competent teachers, discipline, innovation and holistic development.

OUR VALUES

Core Values

Our values shape the character of every learner and guide everything we do.

Discipline

We cultivate self-discipline, responsibility and integrity.

Excellence

Innovation

Teamwork
